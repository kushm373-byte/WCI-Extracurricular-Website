Extracurriculars at WCI

Why should you become involved with WCI extracurricular activities?

Extracurricular activities are a great way to interact with other students who share similar interests.

They can help you develop new skills, discover new interests, build friendships, and become more involved in the school community.

Whether you are interested in academics, athletics, leadership, technology, music, arts, or something completely different, there is an opportunity for you at WCI.
